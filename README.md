## Entry (117 characters)

> Diagnosis: chronic group-chat narrator — you type 'lol' at your own jokes, then wait for applause that never arrives.

## Why it works

- **Real diagnosis shape:** "Diagnosis: chronic ..." reads like a chart note, not a joke pasted into one.
- **The turn is the patient, not the therapist:** the human narrates their own jokes in the group chat and waits for applause that never comes — a behaviour everyone recognises instantly.
- **Quotable and clean:** one image, no setup, no padding; the punchline lands on "applause that never arrives".
- **Fresh angle:** no tab-hoarding, no doomscrolling, no "main-character" line — a group-chat narrator is a diagnosis nobody else in the field used.
